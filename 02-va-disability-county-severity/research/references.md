# References

Verification protocol (Constitution rule 2.4): a source is flagged `verified: yes` only if a page for it was opened in this session and title, authors, year and venue were read from that page. "Claim read" says how far the content, as opposed to the bibliographic record, was checked. Access date: 2026-11-12.

Legend for "claim read": **abstract/page** = the summarised claim was read on the opened page; **metadata only** = the bibliographic record (Crossref API) was opened and matched, but the paper's text/abstract was not opened, so the one-line "supports" is the paper's well-known role and must be re-checked against the text before being quoted.
